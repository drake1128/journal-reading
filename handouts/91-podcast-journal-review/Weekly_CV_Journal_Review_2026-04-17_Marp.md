# OCEANIC-STROKE：Asundexian 中風二級預防
## [DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2513880](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2513880)

- **Phase 3 RCT**：12,327 patients，non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke/TIA within 72h
- **介入**：Asundexian (Factor XIa inhibitor) + antiplatelet vs placebo + antiplatelet
- **追蹤**：2 years

| 終點 | Asundexian | Placebo | HR (95% CI) |
|------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Ischemic Stroke** | **6.2%** | **8.4%** | **0.74 (0.65-0.84)** |
| Disabling/Fatal Stroke | 2.1% | 3.0% | — (31% 降低) |
| ISTH Major Bleeding | 1.9% | 1.7% | 1.10 (0.85-1.44) |

> **Pearl**：Factor XIa 抑制劑選擇性阻斷 intrinsic pathway (pathological thrombosis)，保留 extrinsic pathway (hemostasis) — 首次實現「抗栓不增出血」

---

# OCEANIC-STROKE：臨床意義

- **Intrinsic pathway** → 主導 pathological thrombosis（動脈粥狀硬化處）
- **Extrinsic pathway** → 主導 hemostasis（傷口止血）
- 選擇性抑制 Factor XIa → **分離抗栓與出血風險**

```text
Traditional Anticoagulants     Factor XIa Inhibitors
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━      ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
↓ Thrombosis ✓                ↓ Thrombosis ✓
↑ Bleeding   ✗                Bleeding unchanged ✓
```

- 若獲批准 → 中風二級預防的 **paradigm shift**
- 目前仍在 regulatory review 中

# C-TRACT：Endovascular Therapy for PTS
## [DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2519001](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2519001)

- **Open-label RCT**：225 patients，moderate/severe PTS with iliac-vein obstruction
- **介入**：Iliac vein stenting + enhanced antithrombotic + standard care vs standard care alone

| 終點 | Endovascular | Standard Care | P 值 |
|------|-------------|--------------|------|
| **6-month VCSS** | **8.1 ± 5.1** | **10.0 ± 4.9** | **0.001** |

- 生活品質明顯改善
- 但出血風險較高 — enhanced antithrombotic 所致
- **首個 RCT** 確立 endovascular therapy 在 PTS with iliac-vein obstruction 的角色

# SuDDICU：ICU 選擇性消化道去污染
## [DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2506398](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2506398)

- **Crossover cluster RCT**：多國、多中心

| 終點 | SDD | Standard Care | OR (95% CI) |
|------|-----|--------------|-------------|
| **90-day Mortality** | **27.0%** | **29.1%** | **0.91 (0.82-1.02)** NS |
| MRO Acquisition | 20.9% | 32.5% | Significant |

- 90 天死亡率**未達統計顯著差異**
- 但 multi-resistant organism 獲得率**顯著降低**
- Meta-analysis (32 RCTs): pooled RR 0.91，**99.3% posterior probability of benefit**

> **Pearl**：Bayesian analysis 支持 SDD 有死亡率效益（99.3%），但傳統 frequentist P>0.05 未達顯著 — 解讀取決於統計框架

# LECART：LBBAP vs BiV Pacing for CRT
## [PMID: 41978340](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41978340/)

- **Multicenter RCT**：11 Belgian centers
- **族群**：HF NYHA II-IV, LVEF <40%, LBBB with CRT indication
- **介入**：Left bundle branch area pacing (LBBAP) vs conventional BiV pacing

| 終點 | LBBAP | BiV Pacing | HR / OR | P 值 |
|------|-------|-----------|---------|------|
| **Primary Composite** | **13%** | **25%** | **HR 2.14 (1.01-4.51)** | **0.039** |
| Device Complications | 1% | 15% | OR 6.76 (1.48-31.25) | 0.006 |

- LBBAP **不僅 non-inferior，而且 superior** to BiV pacing
- Complications 僅 1/15 — 免除 coronary sinus lead 相關問題

> **Pearl**：LECART = conduction system pacing 的里程碑。CRT 的 first-line 策略可能需要重新定義。

---

# ULYSSES：US-Guided Puncture for AF Ablation
## [PMID: 41979041](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41979041/)

- **Multicenter RCT**：6 centers, 986 patients
- 試驗因 efficacy 於**第一次 interim analysis 即提前終止**

| 終點 | US-Guided | Conventional | RR (95% CI) |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Primary Composite** | **0.6%** | **3.3%** | **0.19 (0.05-0.63)** |
| 意外動脈穿刺 | 2% | 16% | — |
| 穿刺失敗 (crossover) | 0.2% | 8.2% | — |

- **81% 降低** primary composite complications
- Conventional landmark approach 有 **16% 意外動脈穿刺率** — 2026 年不可接受

> **Pearl**：US guidance 應列為所有 AF ablation 的標準步驟 — standard of care

---

# CAAN-AF：CRT ± AV Node Ablation in AF
## [PMID: 41985940](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41985940/)

- **作者**：Sanders P et al.
- **首個 RCT** 探討 AV node ablation 對 CRT 療效的影響
- **族群**：Permanent AF, LVEF <35%, QRS >120 ms
- **介入**：CRT + AV node ablation vs CRT + medical rate control

**臨床問題**：
- Permanent AF 患者接受 CRT 時，BiV pacing capture rate 常不足
- AV node ablation 可確保 100% pacing capture
- 但長期以來**缺乏 RCT 證據**支持此策略

> **Pearl**：CAAN-AF 填補了 permanent AF + CRT 患者管理的重要知識缺口

# Take-Home Messages

1. **Asundexian (OCEANIC-STROKE)** — Factor XIa 抑制劑降低 26% 中風、不增出血 → paradigm shift
2. **LBBAP (LECART)** — 首個 RCT 證實優於 BiV pacing → CRT first-line 需重新定義
3. **US-guided puncture (ULYSSES)** — 81% 降低併發症 → 應為 AF ablation 標準步驟
4. **SDD (SuDDICU)** — Frequentist NS，但 Bayesian 99.3% probability of benefit → 統計框架影響解讀
5. **C-TRACT** — 首個 RCT 支持靜脈支架治療 PTS with iliac-vein obstruction
6. **AF 數位篩檢** — AI-ECG、Smartwatch 證據持續累積，臨床應用即將到來
7. **GLP-1RA** — 心衰保護效益進一步獲得 target trial emulation 支持
8. **GASS-TAVR** — Geriatric assessment 改善 TAVR patient selection
