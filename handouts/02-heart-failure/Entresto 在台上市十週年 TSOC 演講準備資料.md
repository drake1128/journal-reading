## 1. 藥物開發歷史

### 1.1 從 NEP inhibition 概念到 LCZ696

**核心想法**：
- **Neprilysin (NEP)** 是內肽酶，會水解 natriuretic peptides (ANP, BNP, CNP)、bradykinin、adrenomedullin、substance P 與 angiotensin II (Ang II)
- 抑制 NEP → 內生性 natriuretic peptides ↑ → 利尿、natriuresis、血管擴張、抗交感、抗 RAAS
- **問題**：NEP 抑制同時讓 Ang II 與 bradykinin 升高 → 升壓 + angioedema
- **解法**：把 NEP inhibitor 與 ARB（不是 ACEI！）合併

### 1.2 為什麼不能配 ACEI — Omapatrilat 的教訓

- **Omapatrilat**（vasopeptidase inhibitor，同時抑制 NEP + ACE）於 1990 年代末曾被視為 next big thing
- **OVERTURE trial (2002)**：Omapatrilat 在 chronic HF 對 enalapril 不顯著優越
- **OCTAVE trial (2004)**：在高血壓族群，omapatrilat 的 angioedema 率比 enalapril **高 3 倍** (2.17% vs 0.68%)
- 機轉：ACE + NEP 同時被抑制，bradykinin 累積到危險程度
- FDA 拒絕核准 → omapatrilat 全面退場
- **教訓**：Bradykinin 代謝途徑 (ACE + NEP) 不能同時封死

### 1.3 LCZ696 — Novartis 的 ARNi 設計

- **AHU377 (sacubitril)** — NEP inhibitor prodrug，活化後成 LBQ657
- **Valsartan** — 非 ACEI 的 ARB，避開 bradykinin 累積風險
- 1:1 莫耳比例 supramolecular complex（不是單純混合 — 共結晶 cocrystal）
- 開發代號：**LCZ696**

### 1.4 PARADIGM-HF 之前的概念驗證

- **2010 PARAMOUNT (Phase 2, HFpEF)**：301 名 HFpEF 病人，36 週後 LCZ696 比 valsartan 顯著降 NT-proBNP（pre-specified primary endpoint achieved）— [Solomon SD, et al. Lancet 2012;380:1387-95](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(12)61227-6)
- 這個 phase 2 為後續兩個 phase 3 試驗（PARADIGM-HF 在 HFrEF、PARAGON-HF 在 HFpEF）開門

### 1.5 PARADIGM-HF — 改變遊戲規則的大型試驗

- **設計**：global, double-blind, active-comparator (enalapril) RCT
- **主持人**：John J.V. McMurray, Milton Packer
- **族群**：8,442 名 NYHA II–IV、LVEF ≤35–40% 的 HFrEF 病人
- **試驗早期終止**：median follow-up 27 個月時，DSMB 因 overwhelming benefit 提前停止
- **發表**：[McMurray JJV, et al. NEJM 2014;371:993-1004](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1409077)（2014-09-11 ESC barcelona 同日發表）

**關鍵結果**：
| 終點 | LCZ696 | Enalapril | HR (95% CI) | P |
|------|--------|-----------|-------------|---|
| Primary (CV death + HF hosp) | 21.8% | 26.5% | **0.80 (0.73, 0.87)** | <0.001 |
| All-cause death | 17.0% | 19.8% | 0.84 (0.76, 0.93) | <0.001 |
| CV death | 13.3% | 16.5% | 0.80 (0.71, 0.89) | <0.001 |
| HF hospitalization | — | — | 0.79 | <0.001 |

> **歷史意義**：自 1990 年代 ACEI / β-blocker 被確立為 HFrEF 基石以來，**首次有藥物在 head-to-head 試驗中明確證明優於 enalapril**。

### 1.6 監管核准里程碑

| 日期 | 事件 |
|------|------|
| **2014-09-11** | PARADIGM-HF 結果於 NEJM 發表 + ESC 巴塞隆那同步 |
| 2015-04 | FDA Cardio-Renal Advisory Committee 13-0 通過 |
| **2015-07-07** | **FDA 核准 Entresto** (priority review + fast track) |
| 2015-11-19 | **EMA 核准 Entresto** (歐盟) |
| **~2016-2017** | **TFDA 核准 Entresto 在台灣上市**（奠定本演講十週年基礎） |
| 2019-02 | FDA 標籤更新：擴大至 LVEF "below normal"（涵蓋部分 HFpEF / HFmrEF） |
| 2024-12 | FDA 核准兒科 1 歲以上適應症（基於 PANORAMA-HF） |
| **2025-07** | Entresto core patent 在美國到期，generic 上市 |

### 1.7 為什麼叫 Entresto?

- "Entresto" 來自拉丁文 *en* (in) + *trēs* (three) — 暗指三個機制：NEP 抑制 + RAS 阻斷 + 增強 natriuretic peptides
- 也呼應 *crescere* (grow / restore strength)

## 3. 六大樞紐試驗連續文獻回顧

### 3.1 PARADIGM-HF (2014) — Chronic HFrEF 的基石

（已詳述於 §1.5）

**追加 subgroup 重點**：
- **年齡**：≥75 歲組仍顯著獲益 — Jhund PS, et al. Eur Heart J 2015;36:2576-84
- **女性**：絕對風險降幅與男性相當
- **糖尿病**：HbA1c 下降幅度更大（pleiotropic effect on glucose homeostasis）
- **腎功能**：eGFR 下降速度比 enalapril 慢（renal protection signal）
- **VT/SCD**：appropriate ICD shocks 減少 → 後續引發 anti-arrhythmic effect 假說

### 3.2 PARAGON-HF (2019) — HFpEF 的「險勝/險敗」

**Solomon SD, et al. NEJM 2019;381:1609-20** | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1908655)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 設計 | Global RCT, N=4,822, LVEF ≥45% |
| Primary | Total HF hosp + CV death |
| 結果 | Rate ratio **0.87 (0.75, 1.01)**, **P=0.059** ← **未達統計顯著** |
| Subgroup with benefit | LVEF 45–57%（lower end）+ 女性 |

**意義**：嚴格說 **negative trial**，但 P 接近顯著、subgroup 訊號明顯 → 引導 2019 FDA 核准擴大至 "below normal" LVEF + 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA 指引給 HFpEF Class IIb 推薦

### 3.3 PIONEER-HF (2018) — 急性失代償後院內啟動

**Velazquez EJ, et al. NEJM 2019;380:539-48** | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1812851)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 設計 | RCT, N=881，ADHF 穩定後院內啟動 ARNI vs enalapril |
| Primary | NT-proBNP 8 週時間加權變化 |
| 結果 | ARNI 組 NT-proBNP 降幅 **比 enalapril 多 29%** (P<0.001) |
| 安全性 | 腎功能、低血壓、高血鉀、angioedema 兩組類似 |
| 探索性 | CV death + HF rehosp 顯著降低（HR 0.54, P=0.001）|

> 確立 **「住院期間就可以開始 ARNI」** 的策略，不必等到出院後 4-6 週

### 3.4 TRANSITION (2019) — 在院 vs 出院後啟動的 head-to-head

**Wachter R, et al. Eur J Heart Fail 2019;21:998-1007**

- 開放標籤 RCT, N=1,002
- 比較住院期間 vs 出院後 1-14 天啟動 ARNI
- 兩組 10 週 target dose 達成率類似
- 確立 **早期啟動可行**

### 3.5 PARADISE-MI (2021) — 心肌梗塞後的擴張嘗試

**Pfeffer MA, et al. NEJM 2021;385:1845-55** | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2104508)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 設計 | RCT, N=5,661，AMI + LVEF <40% 或 pulmonary congestion |
| 對照 | Ramipril 5 mg BID（不是 enalapril） |
| Primary | CV death + incident HF |
| 結果 | HR **0.90 (0.78, 1.04)**, **P=0.17** ← **未達顯著** |
| All-cause death | HR 0.88 (0.73, 1.05) — NS |
| 探索性 (total HF events) | win ratio 偏向 ARNI |

> **Negative trial**。臨床啟示：Post-AMI **不建議**以 ARNI 取代 ACEI/ARB 為 routine therapy；只有當病人發展出 HFrEF 才換藥。

### 3.6 PARAGLIDE-HF (2023) — 急性 worsening + LVEF >40%

**Mentz RJ, et al. JAMA 2023;329:1085-95**

- N=466, 急性失代償穩定後 + LVEF >40%
- ARNI vs valsartan
- Primary：8 週 NT-proBNP 變化 — ARNI **降幅更大** (ratio 0.85, P=0.049)
- Pooled analysis with PARAGON-HF (Vaduganathan, EHJ 2023): 整合後 LVEF >40% 也有 modest benefit

## 4. 亞洲族群數據

### 4.1 PARADIGM-HF 中的亞裔次族群分析

- N=1,487 (17.6% of total) Asian patients
- 主要結果與整體一致（HR ~0.80）
- **特殊發現**：
  - Asian 病人 cough 比 enalapril 組少（與全球一致）
  - **Asian 病人低血壓比例較高**（約 2.3 倍 vs enalapril，全球 1.4 倍）
  - 因此亞洲族群建議：**較低起始劑量** (50 mg BID) 並較緩慢 up-titration

### 4.2 PARALLEL-HF (Japan, 2021) — 日本專屬的 phase 3

**Tsutsui H, et al. Circ J 2021;85:584-94** | [PMID 33731544](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33731544/)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 設計 | RCT, N=225, 日本 HFrEF, NYHA II-IV, LVEF ≤35% |
| 對照 | Enalapril 10 mg BID |
| Primary | Time to first CV death or HF hosp |
| 結果 | HR **1.09 (0.65, 1.82)**, P=0.6260 ← **無顯著差異** |
| Secondary | NT-proBNP 顯著早期且持續下降 |
| 安全性 | **低血壓相關 AE** EAIR-based RR **2.3** vs PARADIGM 全球 1.4 |

**詮釋**：
- 亞洲族群（特別是日本）對 ARNI 的 hemodynamic 反應更敏感
- 但統計檢力不足以顯示 hard endpoint 差異（樣本太小）
- 真實世界仍支持使用，但需更謹慎 titration

### 4.3 PREMIER (Japan, 2024) — 急性 HF 院內啟動的亞洲驗證

**Tsutsui H, et al. Eur Heart J 2024;45:4482-92** | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehae504)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 設計 | Investigator-initiated, multicenter, open-label, blinded-endpoint, N=400 |
| 對照 | Standard ACEI/ARB 治療 |
| 介入 | 院內 stabilize 後啟動 ARNI |
| Primary | Week 8 NT-proBNP 變化 |
| 結果 | ARNI 組 -45% vs control -32% (group ratio 0.81) — **顯著有利** |
| 安全性 | 沒有增加 adverse events |

> **亞洲版的 PIONEER-HF** — 確立日本病人「住院 stabilize 後就啟動」的安全性與療效

### 4.4 KorAHF (Korean Acute Heart Failure Registry)

- 在 KorAHF 中分析 sacubitril/valsartan 的「適用性」與「實際使用率」
- 韓國 HFrEF 病人僅 **約 30-40% 符合處方資格**（其餘因低血壓、腎功能、高血鉀等 contraindication 排除）
- 反映亞洲族群在實務上 ARNI 啟動率偏低的結構性問題

### 4.5 台灣 — TSOC HF Registry 2020

- TSOC 2020 啟動的多中心 HF registry，追蹤 2024 年底結束
- **2025 年發布初步結果**（演講重點素材！）
- 核心研究問題：台灣病人 ARNI 處方率、上下調劑量達成率、與西方數據的差異
- 預期可呈現「Entresto 在台灣 10 年的真實世界滲透率」
- **建議演講者直接引用 TSOC registry 數據** — Reference: [PMC10961632](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10961632/)

### 4.6 2023 TSOC 共識 — 慢性 HF 藥物治療台灣本土化建議

- TSOC 2023 共識把 ARNI 列為 **HFrEF first-line therapy**（與美國指引一致）
- 對亞洲病人特別提醒：起始劑量可從 24/26 mg BID 開始 + 緩慢 up-titration
- Reference: [PMC10203721](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10203721/)

## 5. 指引地位演進

| 年份 | 指引 / 共識 | ARNI 地位 |
|------|------------|----------|
| 2016 | ACC/AHA/HFSA HF Update | 首次納入，IIa 推薦取代 ACEI |
| 2016 | ESC HF Guidelines | Class I, Level B（取代 ACEI） |
| 2017 | ACC HFrEF Pathway | "Should be considered" |
| 2021 | ESC HF Guidelines | **Class I, Level B**（HFrEF first-line, four pillars 之一） |
| **2022** | **AHA/ACC/HFSA HF Guideline** | **Class I, Level A for HFrEF**（first-line, 不必先用 ACEI/ARB titration）<br>HFmrEF: Class IIb<br>HFpEF: Class IIb |
| 2023 | TSOC HF Pharmacological Treatment Consensus | First-line for HFrEF |
| 2024 | ACC Expert Consensus Decision Pathway HFrEF | 強調 ARNI + β-blocker + MRA + SGLT2i 「同步啟動」（four pillars） |
| 2025 | ESC HF Update | 維持 Class I HFrEF，對 HFmrEF/HFpEF 隨 PARAGLIDE-HF 數據略升 |

> **十年演進**：從 2014 PARADIGM-HF 結果到 2022 美國指引把 ARNI 提升到「**first-line, 不需 titrate ACEI**」— **8 年完成從 trial 到 first-line therapy 的演進**

## 6. 腎臟科觀點 — CKD 與透析病人

### 6.1 PARADIGM-HF 中的腎功能子群

- 排除條件：eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²
- 基線 eGFR 30–60 之子群仍顯著獲益
- 治療期間 eGFR 下降速度 **比 enalapril 慢** → suggest renal protection

### 6.2 UK HARP-III (2017) — CKD without HF 試驗

- 414 名 eGFR 20-60 的 CKD 病人，無 HF
- ARNI vs irbesartan, 12 個月
- Primary (eGFR change): 兩組類似
- BP 與 albuminuria 改善 → mechanism support

### 6.3 透析病人 — 持續累積的證據

**重要 meta-analysis (2024-2025)**：

#### Meta-analysis #1 (Kidney International Reports 2023)
- 14 studies, 1,236 ESKD patients
- ARNI 在透析族群顯著改善 LVEF（pooled MD +6.58%）
- 降低 HF hospitalization
- 死亡率有 trend 改善但未達顯著

#### Meta-analysis #2 (Annals of Medicine 2025)
**Efficacy and safety of sacubitril/valsartan in ESRD patients with HF: a review**
- [Tandfonline 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07853890.2025.2557515)
- 整合 12 篇研究, 799 病人
- LVEF 改善幅度與 non-ESRD 族群相當
- **高血鉀風險 > 早期 CKD 族群**（這是關鍵安全性訊息）
- SBP 平均下降 -11.09 mmHg、DBP -4.37 mmHg

#### Meta-analysis #3 (Hypertension on dialysis, Pubmed 2025)
- [PMID 39927814](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39927814/)
- 透析病人合併高血壓族群，ARNI 顯著降 BP 而不增 IDH 風險

### 6.4 透析病人臨床建議（演講可用 take home）

> **可以用，但要小心**：
> 1. 起始低劑量 (24/26 mg BID 或更低)
> 2. 透析後給藥（避免 IDH）
> 3. 密集監測 K+、BP
> 4. 不適合：透析中血壓不穩、頻繁 IDH、嚴重心律不整

### 6.5 Heart Failure + ESKD JAHA 2022 真實世界

- [JAHA 2022 — Sacubitril/Valsartan in Patients With HF and ESKD](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.122.026407)
- 觀察性研究確認 ARNI 在 ESKD 族群可改善 LVEF 與 NT-proBNP
- 強化「擴大適應症到透析病人」的觀念

## 7. 瓣膜性心臟病觀點 — MR 病人

### 7.1 PRIME Trial (Korea, 2019) — Functional MR 的關鍵 RCT

**Kang DH, et al. Circulation 2019;139:1354-65** | [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.037077)

| 項目 | 內容 |
|------|------|
| 設計 | 雙盲 RCT, 4 個韓國中心, **N=118** |
| 族群 | HFrEF + chronic functional MR (FMR) |
| 對照 | Valsartan 單藥 |
| Primary | 12 個月時 EROA 變化 |
| 結果 | ARNI 組 EROA **-0.058 cm²** vs valsartan **-0.018 cm²** (P=0.032) |
| Regurgitant volume | ARNI 額外多降 -7.3 mL (P=0.009) |

> **第一個證明 ARNI 改善 functional MR 的 RCT** — 不是直接修瓣膜，而是透過 LV reverse remodeling 間接降低 MR

### 7.2 後續 echocardiography 研究

- **Effect of Sacubitril-Valsartan on Secondary MR (2D + 3D echo)** — [PMID 41729175](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41729175/)

## 9. 上市後安全性監測

### 9.1 EMA Post-Authorization Safety Study (PASS)

- 多資料庫前瞻性 cohort study（5 個歐洲健康資料庫）
- 主要評估：angioedema、hypotension、hyperkalemia、hepatotoxicity、renal impairment
- 結論：與 ACEI/ARB 相比，**主要 risk 是 hypotension 訊號略高**；angioedema 罕見但比 ARB 單藥稍高；**沒有顯著的 hepatotoxicity 訊號**

### 9.2 PARADIGM-HF 試驗中安全性數字

| AE | LCZ696 | Enalapril |
|----|--------|-----------|
| Symptomatic hypotension | **14.0%** | 9.2% |
| Hyperkalemia (K >5.5) | 16.1% | 17.3% |
| Cough | 11.3% | 14.3% |
| Angioedema (黑人風險最高) | 0.5% | 0.2% |
| Renal impairment | 10.1% | 11.5% |

### 9.3 Real-world safety 一致性

- 全球 spontaneous reporting databases（FAERS、EudraVigilance、Japan PMDA）十年數據與試驗 profile 一致
- 沒有 unexpected signal
- 唯一持續關注：**Asian 族群的低血壓 + IDH 訊號**（如前述）

### 9.4 黑框警告 / 重要禁忌

- ❌ 與 ACEI 同時使用 (washout ≥36 小時)
- ❌ 已知 angioedema 病史
- ❌ 懷孕（teratogenic）
- ❌ Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C)
- ❌ 與 aliskiren 在糖尿病病人併用

## 10. Q&A 預備重點

### Q1：兒科病人能用嗎？
✅ 可以，1 歲以上 FDA 已核准（PANORAMA-HF 雖 primary 未顯著，但 safety/efficacy 相當；NT-proBNP 改善）。對 congenital HD 後 systemic LV dysfunction 有 case series 支持；systemic RV 資料少。

### Q2：洗腎病人能用嗎？
✅ 可以使用，2024-2025 多個 meta-analysis 一致顯示對 ESRD/HD 病人 LVEF 改善、HF 住院降低。**但需密集監測**：高血鉀風險高於早期 CKD、IDH 風險、起始用低劑量、透析後給藥。

### Q3：MR 病人能用嗎？
✅ Functional MR 病人有 PRIME trial 直接證據，EROA 顯著降低；建議 TEER 評估前先 GDMT optimization (含 ARNI) 4-6 週。Primary MR (degenerative) 沒有直接 RCT。

### Q4：HFpEF 病人值得用嗎？
🟡 PARAGON-HF 嚴格說 negative (P=0.059)，但 LVEF 45-57% + 女性 subgroup benefit；ACC/AHA 2022 給 Class IIb；可考慮但不是 first-line（first-line 是 SGLT2i）。

### Q5：AMI 後病人需要嗎？
❌ PARADISE-MI negative — 不建議 routine 用 ARNI 取代 ACEI/ARB。只有當病人後續發展為 HFrEF 才換藥。

### Q6：可以與 SGLT2i 一起用嗎？
✅ 推薦！四聯療法 (ARNI + BB + MRA + SGLT2i) 是 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA + 2021 ESC + 2024 ACC Pathway 一致建議。實證來自 DAPA-HF、EMPEROR-Reduced 等試驗的 background therapy 包含大比例 ARNI。

### Q7：黑人病人有差異嗎？
- PARADIGM-HF 中黑人 angioedema 風險偏高（雖整體仍罕見）
- 建議起始劑量低、密切監測
- 仍是 first-line therapy

### Q8：低血壓邊緣的病人怎麼辦？
- 起始劑量 24/26 mg BID
- 從每日改成 every other day 也可
- 確認 baseline SBP ≥100 mmHg 才啟動
- 若 SBP 95-100 mmHg 可考慮但需密切觀察
- 亞洲族群風險相對高（PARALLEL-HF data）

### Q9：和 ACEI/ARB 之間怎麼換？
- ACEI → ARNI：必須 **washout ≥36 hours**（避免 angioedema）
- ARB → ARNI：可以直接換
- ARNI → ACEI：washout ≥36 hours

### Q10：Generic 上市後處方策略？
- 美國 2025-07 patent 到期，generic 已上市
- 台灣 generic 進度可能稍晚 1-2 年
- 對 ARNI 整體可近性是利多
- Brand name 與 generic 在台灣健保給付規則需注意

### Q11：兒科 + congenital HD 的特殊考量？
- Systemic LV dysfunction（DCM、ICM、L-TGA 等）有 PANORAMA-HF 支持
- Systemic RV (atrial switch s/p Mustard/Senning)：case series only
- Single ventricle Fontan 循環：理論上 afterload 降低有益，但臨床數據極少；個別決定

### Q12：對 atrial fibrillation 的影響？
- PARADIGM-HF post hoc：ARNI 組 new-onset AF 略低（HR 0.85, P=0.06，邊緣顯著）
- 機轉：LV reverse remodeling + LA 壓力降低 → 可能 anti-arrhythmic
- 不是建議的 anti-AF therapy，但對 HFrEF + AF 有額外好處

### Block 3 — Mechanism: The Dual Hemodynamic Logic (Slides 4-5) · 3 minutes

**[SLIDE 04 — What Is Sacubitril/Valsartan?]**

Sacubitril/valsartan is the first ARNI — angiotensin receptor-neprilysin inhibitor. The two halves do opposite things in a coordinated way.

**Sacubitril** is a neprilysin inhibitor. Neprilysin is the enzyme that degrades the natriuretic peptides — ANP, BNP, CNP — and bradykinin. So if you block neprilysin, you let the body's *own* protective natriuretic peptides accumulate. They cause sodium excretion, vasodilation, and they have antifibrotic effects on the myocardium and the kidney.

But neprilysin also breaks down angiotensin II. So if you block neprilysin alone, you also raise angiotensin II — which is bad. That is why you need the second half: **valsartan**, the angiotensin receptor blocker. It blocks the AT1 receptor, suppresses the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system, lowers aldosterone, and limits ventricular remodeling.

Why an ARB and not an ACE inhibitor? This is the lesson of **omapatrilat** — the vasopeptidase inhibitor from the late 1990s. It blocked NEP and ACE simultaneously. The result was that bradykinin accumulated dangerously, and angioedema rates were three times higher than enalapril in the OCTAVE hypertension trial. The FDA refused to approve omapatrilat. So the design lesson was: **you can block NEP, but you must spare ACE**. Use an ARB instead. That single design decision is why Entresto exists today.

The metaphor I like to use is: ARNI presses the brake — by suppressing RAAS — and presses the accelerator at the same time — by raising natriuretic peptides. ACE inhibitors and ARBs alone can only do half of that.

**[SLIDE 05 — Foundation of Dual Protection]**

Now, why does this matter so much for the **kidney**? Because the natriuretic peptides are the body's own renal guardians. They cause vasodilation of the *afferent* arteriole, which lowers intraglomerular pressure. Combined with valsartan's vasodilation of the *efferent* arteriole, you get **dual hemodynamic protection** of the glomerulus. Both ends relax, the pressure drops, and the kidney is protected. Add the antifibrotic effect on tubulointerstitial tissue, and you have a molecular rationale for why ARNI helps kidneys — not just hearts. We will see the data on this in a few minutes.

### Block 4 — PARADIGM-HF: The Trial That Changed Everything (Slides 6-7) · 3 minutes

**[SLIDE 06 — PARADIGM-HF data table]**

Now to the landmark. **PARADIGM-HF**, published in NEJM by McMurray, Packer, and colleagues in September 2014. Eight thousand four hundred forty-two patients with HFrEF, NYHA class II to IV, ejection fraction 40 percent or below. Sacubitril/valsartan 200 mg twice daily versus enalapril 10 mg twice daily — a **head-to-head against the gold standard**.

The trial was **stopped early** by the data safety monitoring board, after a median follow-up of 27 months, because the boundary for overwhelming benefit had been crossed.

The numbers — and these you must commit to memory:

- The **primary endpoint** — cardiovascular death plus heart failure hospitalization — hazard ratio **0.80**. A twenty percent relative risk reduction. Number needed to treat: 21.
- **Cardiovascular death** alone — same hazard ratio, 0.80. Twenty percent reduction. NNT 32.
- **All-cause mortality** — hazard ratio 0.84. Sixteen percent reduction. NNT 36.
- **Heart failure hospitalization** — hazard ratio 0.79. Twenty-one percent reduction.

Take a moment with that. Since the 1990s, when we established ACE inhibitors and beta blockers as the foundation of HFrEF therapy, **no drug had been able to beat enalapril head-to-head** on hard outcomes. PARADIGM-HF was the first. From this point forward, ARNI replaced ACEI as first-line therapy. The Class I recommendation followed.

**[SLIDE 07 — Kaplan-Meier curve, PARADIGM-HF primary endpoint]**

Look at this Kaplan-Meier curve. The two arms separate at **month six**, and the gap continues to widen all the way to trial closure. There is no convergence. There is no late catch-up by enalapril. The benefit is early, durable, and consistent. NNT 21 over 27 months — for cardiovascular outcomes in HFrEF, this is among the largest effect sizes we have ever seen.

### Block 5 — From One Trial to a Decade of Trials (Slide 8) · 1.5 minutes

**[SLIDE 08 — From One Trial to a Decade of Trials]**

PARADIGM-HF opened the door, but the field did not stop there. Over ten years, we have asked the molecule six more questions.

- **PIONEER-HF in 2018** — can we start ARNI safely *during* a heart failure admission? Eight hundred eighty-one patients. Answer: yes, NT-proBNP drops 29 percent more than enalapril.
- **UK-HARP-III in 2018** — what about CKD stage 3b to 4? Four hundred fourteen patients. Answer: no harm to the kidney, blood pressure goes down, cardiac biomarkers improve.
- **PARAGON-HF in 2019** — what about HFpEF, ejection fraction 45 percent or higher? 4,822 patients. Answer: trend toward benefit, but the primary endpoint missed at P equals 0.059. Subgroup signal in the lower EF range and in women.
- **PARAGLIDE-HF in 2023** — patients with EF above 40 percent and recent decompensation. NT-proBNP improved versus valsartan, fewer renal events.
- **PREMIER from Japan in 2024** — first major trial in Asian acute heart failure patients. Four hundred patients. We will spend more time on this.
- **And the Doi up-titration sub-analysis in 2025** — patients who actually reached target dose had a fifty-one percent NT-proBNP reduction. **Up-titration is not optional** — that is the key message.

### Block 6 — PIONEER-HF: Starting in the Hospital (Slides 9-10) · 2.5 minutes

**[SLIDE 09 — PIONEER-HF data table]**

Let me focus on PIONEER-HF for a moment. This was a question that mattered to all of us in real practice: can I start sacubitril/valsartan **before discharge**, while the patient is still on the ward, after they have been stabilized from an acute decompensation?

Eight hundred eighty-one patients, in-hospital initiation, sacubitril/valsartan versus enalapril. Look at the numbers.

- **Symptomatic hypotension** — 15 versus 12.7 percent. **Not significant.**

Look at this curve. The separation appears within the first **two weeks** — not at six months, like PARADIGM. Two weeks. By eight weeks the event rates are essentially halved. NNT 13 over eight weeks.

The clinical message is clear: **don't wait until outpatient follow-up**. Start sacubitril/valsartan before the patient leaves the ward, after they have been hemodynamically stabilized. The reverse remodeling clock starts ticking earlier, and the rehospitalization curve bends sooner.

### Block 7 — PREMIER: The First Major Asian AHF Trial (Slides 11-14) · 4 minutes

**[SLIDE 11 — PREMIER · First Major Asian AHF Trial]**

Now, PIONEER-HF was a North American trial. We always have the question — does this apply to **our** patients? Asian patients tend to be smaller, often older, often more sensitive to blood pressure-lowering effects.

Enter **PREMIER**, presented by Tanaka and colleagues at ESC 2024 and published in European Heart Journal in October 2024. A multicenter, randomized, open-label, blinded-endpoint — what we call a PROBE design — trial in **400 hospitalized Japanese patients** with acute heart failure that had been stabilized.

The crucial design choice: **no LVEF restriction**. They enrolled both HFrEF and HFpEF. Mean age was 76 years — a genuinely elderly Asian population. Sixty-one percent were already on an SGLT2 inhibitor. Around 75 percent were on beta blocker and MRA. So this is not a trial of ARNI on top of nothing — this is ARNI on top of **already-modern four-pillar therapy**.

The primary endpoint: 8-week NT-proBNP geometric mean ratio.

**[SLIDE 12 — PREMIER subgroup analyses + Doi up-titration]**

Two important sub-analyses to highlight.

First, the **MRA subgroup**. Patients who were *also* on a mineralocorticoid antagonist at randomization had a *greater* NT-proBNP reduction with ARNI. P equals 0.037 for interaction. So the synergy with MRA is real.

Second — and this is the slide I want you to remember — the **Doi up-titration sub-analysis**, published in Journal of Cardiac Failure in 2025. They split patients into three groups:

- Sacubitril/valsartan **with up-titration to target dose**: NT-proBNP fell **fifty-one percent**. P equals 0.002.
- Sacubitril/valsartan **without up-titration**: NT-proBNP fell **39 percent**. Not significantly different from control.
- ACEI/ARB control: reference.

The take-home is uncomfortable but important: **prescribing the drug is not enough**. You have to titrate to target dose. The biomarker signal in the no-titration group looks essentially the same as the control. **Up-titration is the active ingredient.**

**[SLIDE 13 — PREMIER NT-proBNP trajectory curve]**

Here is the trajectory over eight weeks. Separation at week two. By week eight the geometric mean ratio is 0.69 — a thirty-one percent relative drop. The Doi up-titration group's curve drops even further below.

**[SLIDE 14 — PREMIER safety in Asian AHF]**

And critically — safety. In this elderly Asian population:

- Worsening heart failure or death: **6.2 percent** with ARNI versus 8.5 percent with control. Hazard ratio 0.73.
- Worsening renal function: 19 versus 15 events. Not significant.
- Hyperkalemia, potassium ≥ 5.5: 12 versus 14 events. **No increase**.
- Symptomatic hypotension: 4 versus 9 events. **Numerically lower** with ARNI.

No new safety signal. eGFR with up-titration was actually preserved, and serum potassium did not rise. The conclusion: in an elderly Asian acute heart failure population, **ARNI is safe to up-titrate** — even on top of four-pillar therapy.

### Block 8 — Real-World Evidence: Danish + US Optum (Slides 15-17) · 3 minutes

**[SLIDE 15 — Danish Nationwide RWE · Higher-Risk Patients Benefit Most]**

Trials are one thing. The real world is another. Let me show you two pieces of large-scale real-world evidence that confirm and extend the trial benefits.

First: **Svanström and colleagues**, Danish nationwide cohort, published in American Journal of Cardiology in 2026. Two thousand four hundred forty-six ARNI users matched to nearly five thousand ACEI/ARB users in a one-to-two propensity score design. Period 2018 to 2023.

The headline finding is what I call the **inverse paradox**: the *higher* the baseline risk, the *larger* the absolute risk reduction. Patients with NYHA class III to IV, with very high NT-proBNP, with recent heart failure hospitalization — they have the **largest absolute mortality benefit**.

The implication for our practice: **do not save ARNI for patients who are stable**. The sickest patients have the most to gain.

**[SLIDE 16 — US Optum EHR · De Novo HFrEF — First-Line ARNI]**

Second: **Bhatt and colleagues**, US Optum electronic health record data, published in ESC Heart Failure in 2025. More than nine thousand propensity-score-matched patients with **de novo HFrEF** — meaning their *first ever* prescription for heart failure was either ARNI or a fresh ACEI/ARB.

- All-cause hospitalization: nineteen percent reduction.
- All-cause mortality: **thirty percent reduction**.
- CV hospitalization: twenty percent reduction.
- Heart failure hospitalization: fourteen percent reduction.

This is real-world evidence that supports the 2024 ACC pathway: in the de novo HFrEF patient, **start with ARNI**. Do not start with ACEI and "upgrade" later. The first prescription is the prescription that captures the reverse remodeling window.

**[SLIDE 17 — De Novo Super-Responders · PIONEER-HF + TRANSITION]**

And we have RCT support for this concept too. The **PIONEER-HF de novo subgroup**, published by Ambrosy in JACC 2020, showed that de novo HFrEF patients had a *greater* NT-proBNP reduction and *lower* CV death/HFH than patients with prior HFrEF. The **TRANSITION study** in 2019 confirmed the same pattern: de novo patients show greater NT-proBNP decrease, fewer rehospitalizations, fewer composite events.

So the concept of the **super-responder** — the young patient with newly diagnosed HFrEF, reverse remodeling potential at its maximum — is supported by both RCT subgroup analysis and real-world evidence. One more reason to start early.

### Block 12 — The Renal Evidence: Damman 2024 (Slides 21-22) · 2.5 minutes

**[SLIDE 21 — Glomerular Protection · Dual Hemodynamic mechanism]**

Why are these CKD patients safe? But it is one thing to have a mechanism. It is another to have the evidence. **Damman and colleagues, JACC 2024**, the pre-specified renal analysis of PARADIGM-HF — this is one of the most important papers of the last few years for understanding ARNI in CKD.

It is a real, durable preservation of kidney function over years. The UACR rises slightly, but Damman's interpretation is that this reflects mesangial relaxation — not podocyte injury. The eGFR data is what matters, and the eGFR data is unambiguous.

### Block 18 — Six Case Reports (Slides 36-41) · 9 minutes

**[SLIDE 36 — CASE 01 · HFpEF-PH at systemic-level pressures]**

**Case 01** — published in JACC Case Reports 2025 by Abofrekha and colleagues. A patient with HFpEF and pulmonary hypertension at *systemic-level* pressures. The take-away: **dual neurohormonal therapy**, ARNI plus SGLT2i, started simultaneously and titrated, can transform even severe HFpEF-PH.

**[SLIDE 37 — CASE 02 · HF with impaired ANP secretion in long-standing AF]**

**Case 02** — Kurisu and Fujiwara, Cureus 2024. A patient with long-standing atrial fibrillation. Cardiothoracic ratio 85 percent. Body weight 64 kg. ANP only **46 pg/mL** — *deficient*, because long-standing AF causes left atrial fibrosis, which impairs ANP secretion. NT-proBNP 269.

The take-away: **for the AF + HFpEF patient with impaired ANP secretion, ARNI is mechanistically the right drug** — it amplifies what little endogenous ANP they can still produce.

**[SLIDE 38 — CASE 03 · ARNI sparing surgery]**

**Case 03** — Gerges and colleagues, European Heart Journal Case Reports 2021. A patient with LVEF 15 to 20 percent, severe **functional MR grade IV**, severe TR, NT-proBNP over **9,000 pg/mL**, NYHA IV. The original plan: **CABG plus mitral valve repair plus CRT-D** — a triple cardiac surgery.
